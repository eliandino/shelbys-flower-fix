A little flower therapy

Meet the heart behind the flowers

Hi, I'm Shelby.

After more than 31 years as a cardiology nurse, caring for others has always been at the heart of who I am. My love of flowers and creativity inspired Shelby's Flower Fix, a new way to continue caring for others through beautiful, handcrafted floral designs.

Every creation is thoughtfully designed to celebrate life's special moments, brighten someone's day, or offer comfort during challenging times.
